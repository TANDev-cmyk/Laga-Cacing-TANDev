# 🐍 Laga Cacing.io

**Laga Cacing.io** is a web-based (.io) worm battle game built using **HTML5 Canvas** and **pure JavaScript** without any external libraries. Players will enter a high-stakes, competitive arena to fight against smart AI bots with unique Indonesian local nicknames.

> ⚠️ **CHALLENGE NOTICE:** This is one of the hardest worm games to master! The bots are highly aggressive, and climbing to the top of the leaderboard requires pixel-perfect control and quick reflexes. Are you skilled enough to survive the ultimate battle?

---

## 🎨 Main Features

*   **Ultra Hard Gameplay**: Designed with challenging AI mechanics, making this one of the toughest .io games to achieve the #1 spot.
*   **Modern Neon Visuals**: A sleek black background paired with vibrant neon-blue grid lines that create an immersive arcade vibe.
*   **Real-time Leaderboard**: Track your rank live in the top-right corner as you compete against tough bots like `Bukan_Bot` and `Cacing_Sakti`.
*   **Player HUD Stats**: A clean user interface in the top-left corner to monitor your current status:
    *   ⭐ **Score**: Total points gathered from energy pellets.
    *   🏆 **Level**: Your current worm progression level.
    *   ⚡ **Boost**: Your remaining instant speed percentage.
*   **Radar Navigation (Mini-map)**: A handy radar in the bottom-right corner to scan and track enemy positions across the map.
*   **Diverse Worm Skins**: Features beautifully detailed worm designs, including bright neon-green, striped neon-blue, and aggressive red-and-black patterns.

---

## 🎮 How to Play

1.  **Steer Your Worm**: Move your mouse cursor or touch controls to direct the worm's head.
2.  **Collect Energy**: Eat the colorful glowing pellets scattered across the arena to increase your score and level up.
3.  **Activate Boost**: Use your *Boost* meter for a sudden burst of speed to chase down food or trap your opponents.
4.  **Survive the Arena**: Avoid crashing into other worms' bodies at all costs. Outsmart the aggressive AI and claim the crown!
